# Patent Intelligence Workflow

Patents → Technology Trends → Competitor Intelligence → Business Decisions

## 1. Patents
Raw filings from Espacenet: title, assignee, inventors, abstract, CPC codes and dates. For III-V lasers, this is the evidence of who is inventing what.

## 2. Technology Trends
Counting and clustering patents over time shows which laser technologies are growing, such as quantum dot or VCSEL designs, and which are fading.

## 3. Competitor Intelligence
Linking trends to companies like Coherent, Lumentum and Trumpf shows who is investing where, and which portfolios are getting stronger.

## 4. Business Decisions
The output informs licensing, R&D priorities, acquisitions and, most importantly for us, which companies could become clients for our III-V lasers.
